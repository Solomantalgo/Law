# R. Mackay Advocates — stationery concept

## What “headed paper” means

Headed paper (also called letterhead) is the firm's branded page used for letters, legal correspondence, invoices and other documents. The printed or digital header identifies the firm; a restrained footer can carry address and contact details. The large open area is deliberately left for the letter itself. This preview shows a first-page letterhead, not a completed legal letter.

## Brand direction

Use the existing site's visual language: deep navy (`#071A2F`), warm muted gold, generous white space and a classic serif wordmark/headline supported by a clean sans-serif for contact details. Keep the logo prominent but not oversized. Use the gold as a fine rule or small accent, not as a large background. The overall tone should feel established, clear and quietly confident.

## QR code recommendation

Use a QR that points to a page on the firm's own domain, ideally a short branded route such as `/connect` or `/contact`. That page can offer tap-to-call, email, directions, and a downloadable vCard, and it can be updated without reprinting the QR. A Linktree-style page is only preferable if the firm has many frequently changing destinations and chooses to maintain that third-party account. The site content reviewed here does not establish a dedicated digital business-card route, so verify the destination and test the final QR before print. Do not rely on the decorative QR squares in the generated concept board; they are nonfunctional.

## Suggested production tools

- **Visual direction:** these generated concept boards, alongside the existing HTML mockup.
- **Final editable letterhead:** Microsoft Word or Google Docs template for everyday correspondence, with a print-ready PDF for the printer. This makes the body area easy for staff to use.
- **Final business card:** Canva is practical for layout and review; use the printer's exact dimensions/bleed setup and export print PDF. Illustrator or InDesign are good when a print shop requests professional prepress files.

## How to use and develop the samples

1. Open `samples.html` in a browser to review the concepts. Use browser print preview at 100% scale to inspect their proportions.
2. Ask the client which direction they prefer and confirm who needs cards, each person's title, direct phone/email, and any required professional or registration wording.
3. Confirm the printer's paper size, bleed, safe area, stock, finish, and color requirements before preparing production artwork. The letterhead uses A4 proportions; the card is shown at 90 × 50 mm, a common regional format, but the printer should confirm.
4. Build the final print files in a layout/design application with embedded or outlined fonts, a high-resolution/print-ready logo, CMYK colors, and printer-specified bleed. Keep the letterhead body area clear and check that contact details remain legible at actual size.
5. Approve a physical proof before ordering a full print run. Export a separate lightweight PDF letterhead for digital correspondence.
